Matchbook

Privacy Policy

Effective July 9, 2026

Matchbook is a wingperson app: people with accounts (“wingpeople”) create profiles for their single friends and make introductions. This policy explains what we collect from each kind of person who touches Matchbook, why, and how to get it deleted. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to make introductions work, we don’t run ads, and we don’t sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

If you have a Matchbook account (wingpeople)

  • Account info: your email address and password, or your Apple ID credential if you use Sign in with Apple, plus the display name you choose. Your display name is shown to people you introduce (“Lia vouches for this”).
  • Your roster: the profiles you build: names, ages, photos, and everything else you write about your friends.
  • Push token: a device token so we can notify you when someone wants to meet one of your friends. It’s optional; deny the permission and everything still works.

If a friend put you on their roster (singles)

Profiles are created by your friend, and Matchbook only serves a profile after your friend confirms you know you’re on it. Your profile page is reachable only by an unguessable secret link that your friend chooses who to share with. It is never listed, searchable, or indexed. If you want off anyway, tell us and we’ll remove the profile, or ask your friend to delete it (removal is instant and also permanently ends any chats).

If you’re chatting (guests and singles)

Chats are account-free. We store the messages you send so the other person can read them, and nothing else about you: no name, no phone number, no account. Access is through your secret link alone. Either person can type STOP (or tap End chat) to end the chat permanently. The wingperson who made the introduction can never read your messages.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, no ad tracking, no analytics trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • No selling or renting data to anyone, ever.
  • No scanning your contacts, location, or anything else on your device.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication, and photo storage) and the web pages are served by Netlify. Push notifications are delivered through Expo and Apple. These providers process data on our behalf and can’t use it for their own purposes.

Deleting your data

  • Wingpeople: Settings → Delete account, inside the app. This permanently deletes your account, every profile you created, all photos, and every chat and message tied to them. It cannot be undone.
  • Singles: ask your friend to remove your profile, or contact us and we’ll take it down.
  • Chat participants: type STOP to end a chat permanently. To have a chat’s contents erased, contact us with your chat link.

Age

Matchbook is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and every profile on a roster must be of someone 18 or older.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU or California), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data. Email us and we’ll honor them, no forms, no fuss.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes materially, we’ll note it here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: cutiechoi@gmail.com.
